Kora fire

Complex tools, made simple for artists.

Co-developer of Kora, the volumetric combustion solver behind the large-scale fire and explosions of Avatar: Fire and Ash.

The aim was simple to state and hard to build: a physically grounded solver that scales to whole-sequence fire, wrapped in a Houdini toolset artists can drive shot to shot without an engineer beside them.
